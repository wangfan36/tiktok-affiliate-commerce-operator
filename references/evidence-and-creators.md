# Evidence, creators, and winning patterns

Use this reference for product deep dives, creator matching, external intelligence,
and competitor-video analysis.

## Source hierarchy

| Level | Source | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Official/first-party | Current TikTok policy, product page, seller listing, account analytics | Policy, eligibility, current product facts, own performance |
| 2 — Independently supported | Transparent research, public filings, credible datasets, several independent confirmations | Market structure and directional conclusions |
| 3 — Named provider/operator | FastMoss, identifiable sellers, creators, agencies, service providers | Candidate discovery and testable operating signals |
| 4 — Unverified | Anonymous anecdotes, screenshots without context, marketing claims | Low-cost hypothesis only |

For every material current claim record the direct source, event/effective date,
publication or update date, access date, market, category, metric definition, and
conflict of interest. A new article about an old event is not a new signal. Repeated
coverage is new evidence, a signal update, no material change, or a duplicate—not
automatically a new opportunity.

Current policy, prohibited-product, claims, AI-disclosure, eligibility, and
enforcement conclusions must be verified against the relevant official US TikTok
page. Provider estimates cannot override official rules.

## Product-to-creator workflow

For each of at most five final products:

1. Use `product_creator_analysis`, ordered by product units or product GMV, to map
   the creator structure and identify repeat contributors.
2. Shortlist creators using current product contribution, recent commerce activity,
   content fit, and audience fit—not followers alone.
3. For at most three creators per product, use `creator_fans_distribution` and
   `creator_video_analysis` to inspect US audience segments, content tags, linked
   products, selling results, and interaction evidence.
4. Preserve the distinction between creator-video performance and product
   performance. A high-play video is not proof of attributable orders.

Creator assessment should cover:

- product/category fit and prior relevant sales;
- audience location, age, gender, and likely use occasion;
- recent 28-day commerce activity rather than only lifetime totals;
- product concentration and dependence on one viral post;
- repeat posting, content volume, and fatigue risk;
- visual format compatibility with AI-assisted faceless or UGC production;
- evidence gaps and contact/sample costs needed for a profit decision.

Do not expose or persist personal email addresses, phone numbers, private messages,
or other contact data. Discovery does not authorize outreach or sample shipment.

## Audience synthesis

When asked for a product audience, separate:

- **Observed audience:** provider or account demographic data.
- **Inferred buyer:** likely motivations, situations, objections, and content habits.
- **Unknown:** missing psychographic or purchase evidence.

For combined profiles, label a union as broad coverage and an intersection as the
shared core. Do not turn an inferred persona into a verified demographic fact.

## Winning-pattern deconstruction

Use `product_video_list` and creator video evidence to extract reusable commercial
logic:

```text
Buyer/use occasion → pain or desired state → first visible action
→ product reveal → visible proof → objection/offer → CTA
```

Record hook type, opening frame, spoken promise, on-screen label, proof action,
product exposure timing, duration, edit rhythm, CTA, visible evidence, and measured
outcome. Mark attribution limitations.

Replicate only abstract patterns such as problem order, proof structure, pacing,
camera role, and CTA logic. Produce independent scenes, wording, actors, audio, and
visual expression. Never copy a creator's identity, voice, script, shot sequence,
music, watermark, or protected footage. Do not download or reuse media unless the
rights and intended commercial use are clear.

## Intelligence-to-decision contract

For a material signal, report:

```text
Signal → Evidence level → Business implication → Hypothesis
→ Smallest falsifiable test → Decision threshold → Action
```

Valid operating actions are `TEST`, `SCALE`, `PAUSE`, `MONITOR`, `INVESTIGATE`, and
`RETIRE`. An operator claim or isolated screenshot may justify a reversible test,
but never major spend or a scale decision.
